# iOS home

- The iOS home tab is the shared home feed: the hints, the QR button that
  opens the pass and the My ITMO button, with «Недоступно в демо» in the demo.
- On iOS the widget hint's «Добавить» explains how to add a widget («Как
  добавить виджет»), the notification hint's «Включить» asks iOS or opens the
  app's notification settings, and the new-marks card stays hidden until mark
  tracking ships on iOS.
