# iOS calendar

- The iOS app keeps the own schedule in the phone's calendar: «Синхронизация
  с календарём» asks for full calendar access, creates the calendar
  «ITMO.Widgets» in iCloud (or on the phone) and updates it every two hours
  and on return to the app, as on Android; a refusal for good offers
  «Открыть настройки».
- «Выгрузить в .ics» works on iOS: the same ranges and states, and
  «Отправить» opens the share sheet with the `.ics` file.
