# iOS recordbook

- The iOS app shows «Зачётка» as the first tab, as on Android: the shared
  list with its period picker in a native sheet, the BARS chip and the BARS
  sign-in sheet; in the demo the recordbook is Anna's and BARS says
  «Недоступно в демо».
- A subject opens the shared subject page on iOS with its result card, control
  points, teachers and nearest lessons; a teacher opens the profile, the sheet
  total opens the table in Safari and «Изменить итог» opens «Мои баллы» in a
  native sheet.
