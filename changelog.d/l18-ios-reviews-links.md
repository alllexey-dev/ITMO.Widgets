# iOS reviews and subject links

- The iOS subject page shows «Ссылки» and «Чаты» as on Android: a link opens
  outside the app, a long press shows its actions, «Все ссылки» and «Добавить
  ссылку» open the shared sheets in native iOS sheets, and the sheet total can
  be connected from a table link.
- A link's actions vote, pin, copy («Ссылка скопирована»), edit, delete and
  report it; «Пожаловаться» opens «Жалоба на ссылку» over the actions.
- A teacher's profile on iOS shows the reviews with their AI summary, and the
  teacher rows carry their tone; «Написать» opens the review editor, which asks
  «Не сохранять отзыв?» before unsaved changes are dropped, and a review's
  «Пожаловаться» opens «Жалоба на отзыв».
- In the demo every change says «Недоступно в демо» and nothing reaches
  Backend.
