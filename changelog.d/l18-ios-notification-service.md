# iOS notification service

- The iOS notification service books the sport place a push announces, as
  Android's push handler does: «Вы записаны на спорт» or «Не удалось записать
  на спорт» with the section and the start, the queue settled or cancelled on
  Backend; a full lesson shows nothing, and when My ITMO does not answer the
  push keeps «Освободилось место на занятии». It refreshes the session under
  the app's lock and never books for another account, the demo or without the
  services opt-in.
