# iOS schedule

- The iOS schedule tab is the shared schedule: the days from today with the
  minute ticker, «Расписание друзей» and the selected friend with «Сменить»
  and «Вернуться к своему»; the `today` shortcut, quick action and widget tap
  bring the own schedule back on today.
- On iOS a lesson opens its sheet as on Android, from the schedule, home and
  another user's schedule: the change, the meeting link, the note and
  «Друзья на паре»; «Открыть на карте» hands the building to Apple Maps.
- The friend picker, the found schedule changes from the home card, another
  user's schedule from a profile and the queued sport sheet open on iOS; the
  home feed shows the schedule and changes cards.
- Until their iOS cards ship, iOS shows no teacher tone dot, no queued sport
  rows and no calendar sync, and the schedule change check now runs in the
  background with the schedule data.
