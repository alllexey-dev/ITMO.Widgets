# iOS sport

- The iOS sport tab is the shared one: «Мой спорт» with the score and the
  bookings, «Запись» with the filters, the section dialog and the week strip;
  in the demo a booking says «Недоступно в демо».
- On iOS a booking or lesson opens its details in a native sheet, «Поделиться»
  sends the lesson's link through the system share sheet, a building opens in
  Apple Maps, and a sport link or notification opens the lesson in «Запись».
- Another user's sport opens on iOS from their profile, and the sport card
  shows on the iOS home feed.
