# iOS social and Me

- The iOS me tab is the shared Me tab: the profile with «Поделиться», friends
  with the request badge, «Найти людей», privacy, «Вход на сайт», settings,
  the project links and «Выйти» with its confirmation; iOS has no developer
  tools row.
- Friends, people search, the person profile and another user's friends open
  on iOS as on Android, from the Me tab, the home friend-requests card and a
  friendship notification; in the demo a friendship action says «Не
  получилось: Недоступно в демо».
- Copying the ISU on iOS confirms with «Номер ИСУ скопирован».
